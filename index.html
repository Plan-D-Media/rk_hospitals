About the hospital

Quality specialist care, kept close to Barrackpore

Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital is a unit of Sarbani Hospitality and HealthCare Services Pvt Ltd. It was founded by Sarbani Das, who brings 18 years in hospitality to the way patients and families are looked after here.

The hospital was started with one aim: make quality, specialist healthcare accessible to families in Barrackpore — the kind of care that usually meant a trip into central Kolkata. From emergencies to planned surgery and everyday check-ups, more of it now happens close to home.

  • Specialist-led departments across 16+ specialities
  • Round-the-clock emergency, critical care and diagnostics
  • Cashless treatment for government schemes, PSUs and TPAs
