Urology in Barrackpore

Assessment and treatment of kidney, bladder and urinary tract conditions — with pathology and CT imaging in-house, and a dialysis unit on the same campus.

Kidney, bladder & urinary care CT imaging on site Dialysis unit on campus

Overview

The urology department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital assesses and treats conditions of the kidneys, bladder, urinary tract and the male reproductive system. Kidney stones, urinary infections, difficulty passing urine and prostate problems are among the conditions seen most often.

Pathology and CT imaging are available in the hospital itself, which matters for a specialty where the diagnosis often turns on a scan and a urine or blood result. For patients whose kidneys have failed, the hospital's dialysis unit — including a separate isolation facility — is on the same campus.

Frequently asked questions

Can kidney stones be investigated here?

Yes. CT scanning and pathology are available in the hospital's own departments in Barrackpore, and these are the investigations most often used to find and assess kidney stones.

Is dialysis available if my kidneys have failed?

Yes. The hospital has a haemodialysis unit on the same campus, including a separate isolation facility for patients who test positive for a transmissible infection.

Do you treat prostate problems?

Yes. Prostate problems, including difficulty passing urine, are among the conditions assessed and managed by the urology department. Your doctor will advise on investigation and treatment after examining you.

What should I do about blood in my urine?

Blood in the urine should always be assessed by a doctor, even if it happens once and settles. Book a urology consultation. If it is heavy, or comes with severe pain or fever, come to the 24×7 emergency department.

Is treatment in this department available cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government health schemes, PSU empanelments and the major TPAs the hospital is empanelled with. Please carry your scheme or insurance details so the desk can verify eligibility before admission.
