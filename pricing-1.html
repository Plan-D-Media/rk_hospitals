Cashless & empanelment

Cashless, TPA & Corporate / PSU Empanelment

Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital is empanelled with government health schemes, public-sector undertakings and major TPAs — so eligible patients can be treated cashless, without paying up front and claiming later.

Cashless & TPA PSU & corporate tie-ups Government health schemes

How cashless treatment works

Cashless treatment means an eligible patient does not have to pay the hospital directly for covered services. Instead, the hospital coordinates with your insurer, TPA or scheme, and the approved amount is settled between them. You simply carry the right documents and let our desk verify your eligibility.

Cashless is available for planned admissions and, where your policy or scheme allows, for emergencies. Any items that fall outside your cover, or amounts above approved limits, are payable by the patient — our team will explain this clearly before you proceed.

What is a TPA?

A Third-Party Administrator (TPA) is the agency that processes cashless requests and claims on behalf of an insurer. When you come for treatment, our billing desk raises a request to your TPA with your details and the treatment plan, and works with them on approvals during your stay.

Corporate & PSU tie-ups

Many public-sector undertakings (PSUs), government establishments and corporates have empanelled Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital for the treatment of their employees and dependants. Depending on the organisation's arrangement, care may be provided cashless or on a reimbursement basis using the referral or entitlement documents issued by your employer.

If your organisation is not listed below, please check with our front desk — new tie-ups are added over time, and reimbursement may still be possible under your organisation's medical policy.

What to carry for a cashless admission

  • Your health scheme card or health insurance policy details.
  • Photo ID of the patient (and of the primary policyholder, if different).
  • For PSU / corporate patients: the referral letter, entitlement card or authorisation from your employer.
  • The TPA / insurer ID number, and any pre-authorisation reference if you already have one.
  • Doctor's advice or referral for the planned admission, along with earlier reports if available.

Bringing these on arrival helps our desk raise the cashless request and pre-authorisation quickly. Approval timelines and covered amounts are decided by your insurer, TPA or scheme, not by the hospital.

Please note: empanelment and cashless facilities can change over time and are subject to the terms of your scheme, insurer or employer. For the current position on any scheme or TPA, please confirm with our billing desk.
