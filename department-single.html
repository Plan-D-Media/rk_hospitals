Cardiology in Barrackpore

Assessment, monitoring and follow-up for heart conditions — with Echo, ECG, Holter and TMT done in-house, backed by 24×7 emergency and critical care.

Echo · ECG · Holter · TMT 24×7 emergency support Cashless & TPA

Overview

The cardiology department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital looks after the heart health of families in and around Barrackpore — from a first check-up for chest discomfort to ongoing monitoring of a known heart condition. Because the common cardiac tests are available on site, your doctor can assess, diagnose and plan treatment without sending you across the city.

Cardiac care here is supported by the hospital's 24×7 emergency department and critical care units, so urgent problems can be handled quickly and, where needed, admitted for closer observation.

Conditions we assess

Chest pain & palpitations
High blood pressure (hypertension)
Irregular heartbeat (arrhythmia)
Breathlessness on exertion
Follow-up after a cardiac event
Pre-surgical cardiac fitness

Tests & investigations available

  • Echocardiography (Echo) — ultrasound imaging of the heart's structure and pumping.
  • ECG — a quick recording of the heart's electrical activity.
  • Holter monitoring — continuous ECG over 24–48 hours to catch intermittent problems.
  • Treadmill Test (TMT) — checks how the heart behaves under exertion.

Frequently asked questions

What cardiac tests are available here?

Echocardiography (Echo), ECG, Holter monitoring and Treadmill Test (TMT) are all available in-house, so most heart assessments and follow-ups can be completed at the hospital in Barrackpore.

Do you handle cardiac emergencies at night?

Yes. The emergency department runs 24×7 with triage and is backed by ICU and HDU, so cardiac emergencies can be assessed and stabilised at any hour.

Is cardiology treatment available cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government schemes, PSU empanelments and major TPAs. Please carry your scheme or insurance details so the desk can verify eligibility.
