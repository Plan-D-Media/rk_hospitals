In-house Diagnostics in Barrackpore

Tests, imaging and reports done on site — so you get answers without being sent across the city. Pathology, endoscopy, cardiac tests, neurology studies and radiology up to CT, all backed by 24×7 emergency and critical care.

Pathology & lab Radiology up to CT 24×7 emergency support

Diagnostics without the running around

Most of the tests your doctor needs are available in-house at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital. That means fewer trips between centres, quicker turnaround on reports and a treating team that can act on results the same day. From routine blood work to a CT scan, the common investigations for families in and around Barrackpore are handled on the same campus.

Because diagnostics sit alongside the hospital's 24×7 emergency department and critical care units, urgent tests can support the team looking after you at any hour of the day or night.

Our in-house diagnostics

Each of the services below is available on site. Your consulting doctor will advise which tests are right for you.

Pathology

Blood and laboratory investigations to support diagnosis, monitoring and health check-ups.

Diagnostic Endoscopy

Upper GI endoscopy and therapeutic scopes to examine and treat the digestive tract.

PFT & Bronchoscopy

Pulmonary function testing to assess breathing, plus bronchoscopy for the airways.

Neurology

EEG, NCV and EMG studies to assess the brain, nerves and muscle function.

Cardiology

Echocardiography (Echo), ECG, Holter monitoring and Treadmill Test (TMT) for the heart.

Radiology up to CT Scan

X-ray and CT scan imaging on site, so most radiology can be done at the hospital.

Imaging on site

Radiology runs from routine X-rays through to CT scans, supporting emergency, surgical and out-patient care. Having imaging in-house means your doctor can move from question to answer quickly, without arranging a separate appointment elsewhere.

Frequently asked questions

Is a CT scan available in-house?

Yes. The hospital has radiology up to CT scan on site, along with X-ray, so your CT can be done here in Barrackpore without being sent to another centre.

Which diagnostic tests are done in-house?

In-house diagnostics cover pathology (blood and lab work), diagnostic endoscopy, pulmonary function tests and bronchoscopy, neurology studies (EEG, NCV, EMG), cardiac tests (Echo, ECG, Holter, TMT) and radiology up to CT scan.

Are diagnostics available during an emergency?

Yes. With diagnostics on site and a 24×7 emergency department backed by ICU and HDU, urgent tests such as pathology, ECG and radiology can support the emergency team at any hour.
