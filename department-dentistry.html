Dentistry & Oral Surgery in Barrackpore

Dental care for the whole family, including oral and maxillofacial surgery — with the hospital's diagnostics and 24×7 emergency department behind it.

Dental care Oral & maxillofacial surgery 24×7 emergency support

Overview

The dentistry department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital treats the everyday problems of teeth and gums, and, because it is part of a multispeciality hospital, the more involved problems of the mouth, jaws and face that a standalone clinic would have to refer on.

Oral and maxillofacial surgery covers the surgical treatment of the mouth, jaws and face — impacted wisdom teeth, facial injuries, jaw problems and oral swellings. Facial injuries arriving through the hospital's 24×7 emergency department can be assessed by the same team, with X-ray and CT imaging available on site.

Conditions we treat

Toothache and tooth decay
Gum problems
Impacted wisdom teeth
Jaw pain and jaw joint problems
Facial injuries
Oral swellings and lesions

Procedures & services offered

  • Dental consultation and examination — for adults and children.
  • Tooth extraction — including surgical removal of impacted teeth.
  • Oral and maxillofacial surgery — surgical treatment of the mouth, jaws and face.
  • Management of facial injuries — with support from the 24×7 emergency department.
  • Assessment of oral swellings and lesions
  • X-ray and CT imaging on site — when imaging of the jaws or face is needed.

Frequently asked questions

What is oral and maxillofacial surgery?

Oral and maxillofacial surgery is the surgical treatment of conditions of the mouth, jaws and face. It covers impacted wisdom teeth, facial injuries, jaw problems and swellings or lesions in the mouth. It is a recognised specialty requiring further training after a dental degree.

Who is the dental surgeon at the hospital?

Dr. Aniket Sarkar (BDS, MDS in Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery) consults in the dentistry department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital, Barrackpore. Book an appointment and our team will call you back to confirm a slot.

Can a facial injury be treated here?

Yes. Facial injuries can be assessed through the hospital's 24×7 emergency department, with X-ray and CT imaging available on site and the oral and maxillofacial surgery team in the same hospital.

Do you remove impacted wisdom teeth?

Yes. Surgical removal of impacted wisdom teeth is one of the procedures carried out by the oral and maxillofacial surgery service. Your surgeon will examine you and explain what is involved before anything is planned.

Is treatment in this department available cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government health schemes, PSU empanelments and the major TPAs the hospital is empanelled with. Please carry your scheme or insurance details so the desk can verify eligibility before admission.
