About the hospital

About Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital in Barrackpore

A multispeciality hospital built to keep serious care close to home — with 24×7 emergency, complete critical care and in-house diagnostics, right on Ghoshpara Road.

16+ specialities 24×7 emergency with triage Barrackpore, Kolkata

Who we are

Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital is a unit of Sarbani Hospitality and HealthCare Services Pvt Ltd, serving families in and around Barrackpore, Kolkata.

The hospital was founded by Sarbani Das, who came to healthcare after 18 years in the hospitality industry. That background shapes how the hospital is run: clinical care comes first, but so does the way patients and families are looked after through what is often a difficult and anxious time.

The idea behind the hospital was straightforward. For years, families in Barrackpore had to travel into central Kolkata for specialist treatment, emergencies and even routine investigations. Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital was started to change that — to bring emergency care, critical care, specialist departments and diagnostics together on one campus, so more of that care can happen close to home.

Our mission

To make quality healthcare accessible and affordable for the Barrackpore community — bringing emergency, specialist and diagnostic care together under one roof, so families don't have to travel far for the treatment they need.

Our vision

To be the hospital that families in and around Barrackpore turn to first — trusted for round-the-clock emergency and critical care, and for treating every patient with the attention and dignity they deserve.

Why families choose us

24×7 emergency with triage

An emergency department that runs around the clock and sorts the most urgent cases first.

Complete critical care

ICU and HDU for adults, plus NICU and PICU for newborns and children — all on site.

In-house diagnostics up to CT

Pathology, endoscopy, cardiac tests and radiology up to CT, so results come back faster.

Cashless & TPA

Local & affordable
