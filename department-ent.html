ENT in Barrackpore

Assessment and treatment of ear, nose and throat problems, with microscope-assisted examination and CT imaging available on site.

Microscope-assisted examination CT imaging on site 24×7 emergency support

Overview

The ENT department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital treats conditions of the ear, nose and throat for adults and children. Examinations are carried out with microscope assistance, which gives the doctor a magnified, well-lit view of the ear canal and eardrum — important when a diagnosis turns on small details.

Where imaging is needed, CT scanning is available in the hospital's own radiology department, so scans of the sinuses or the ear can be arranged without sending you elsewhere. Children can be seen with the support of the paediatrics department on the same campus.

Conditions we treat

Ear pain, discharge and difficulty hearing
Blocked nose and sinus problems
Sore throat and tonsil problems
Vertigo and balance problems
Snoring and breathing difficulty during sleep
A foreign body in the ear, nose or throat

Procedures & services offered

  • Microscope-assisted ear examination — a magnified view of the ear canal and eardrum.
  • Nose and throat examination — and assessment of persistent symptoms.
  • Medical management — of ear, nose, throat and sinus conditions.
  • CT imaging on site — for the sinuses and the ear when a scan is needed.
  • Removal of foreign bodies — from the ear, nose or throat.
  • Support from the 24×7 emergency department — for urgent ENT problems.

Frequently asked questions

What is a microscope-assisted ear examination?

It is an examination of the ear carried out using an operating microscope rather than a hand-held instrument alone. The magnification and lighting let the doctor see the ear canal and eardrum clearly, which helps in diagnosing infections, perforations and other problems.

Can I get a CT scan of my sinuses here?

Yes. CT scanning is available in the hospital's own radiology department in Barrackpore, so imaging of the sinuses or the ear can be arranged on site rather than at a separate centre.

Do you treat children in the ENT department?

Yes. Children are seen in the ENT department, with the hospital's paediatrics department and, if needed, its paediatric intensive care unit on the same campus.

What should I do if something is stuck in my ear or nose?

Do not try to remove it yourself, as this often pushes the object further in. Come to the hospital — the ENT department can remove foreign bodies, and the emergency department is open 24×7 if it happens outside OPD hours.

Is treatment in this department available cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government health schemes, PSU empanelments and the major TPAs the hospital is empanelled with. Please carry your scheme or insurance details so the desk can verify eligibility before admission.
