24×7 Emergency & Trauma Care

An emergency department that runs around the clock with triage, resuscitation and critical care backup — so serious problems are seen quickly, close to home in Barrackpore.

Open 24×7 with triage ICU & HDU backup In-house diagnostics up to CT

Overview

The emergency department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital is open 24 hours a day, every day, for accidents, sudden illness and medical emergencies. When you arrive, patients are triaged — assessed and sorted so the most urgent cases are attended to first — and then stabilised and treated by the on-duty emergency team.

Because the hospital has critical care units and diagnostics on the same campus, an emergency can move quickly from assessment to imaging, admission or specialist referral without transferring you elsewhere.

What emergency & trauma care covers

Triage & rapid assessment on arrival
Resuscitation & stabilisation of critical patients
Accident & trauma / injury care
Acute medical emergencies
Wound care, dressings & minor procedures
Admission to ICU / HDU when needed

Backed by critical care & diagnostics

Emergency care here does not stand alone. The department is supported by the hospital's wider services so that a patient can be moved seamlessly to the next step of care:

  • Critical care backup — ICU and HDU for adults, plus NICU and PICU for newborns and children, for patients who need close monitoring.
  • In-house diagnostics — pathology, ECG and radiology up to CT scan, so key tests can be done without delay.
  • Specialist support — referral to cardiology, orthopaedics, surgery and other departments on the same campus.

When to come to the emergency department

Come straight to emergency — or call first — for warning signs that should not wait for a routine OPD visit, such as:

Chest pain or pressure, or severe palpitations
Sudden or severe breathlessness
Serious injury, major bleeding or a suspected fracture
Sudden weakness, slurred speech or loss of consciousness
High fever in an infant or a very unwell child
Severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting or dehydration

This list is a general guide, not medical advice. If you are unsure, it is safer to call or come in.

Ambulance

If you need help reaching the hospital, call our emergency line and the team can guide you on the fastest way in.

Frequently asked questions

Is the emergency department really open 24×7?

Yes. The emergency department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital runs around the clock, every day, with triage so the most urgent patients are seen first.

What happens when I arrive in an emergency?

You are triaged and assessed by the on-duty team, given immediate treatment to stabilise you, and — where needed — sent for tests, admitted to ICU/HDU or referred to a specialist on the same campus.

Can emergency treatment be cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government schemes, PSU empanelments and major TPAs. Please share your scheme or insurance details at the desk so the team can verify eligibility.
