Dialysis in Barrackpore

Haemodialysis for patients with kidney failure, including a separate isolation facility for positive patients — with critical care and in-house pathology alongside.

Haemodialysis Separate isolation facility 24×7 emergency support

Overview

The dialysis unit at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital provides haemodialysis for patients whose kidneys can no longer clear the blood on their own. Sessions are provided both for patients on maintenance dialysis and for patients admitted to the hospital who need dialysis as part of their treatment.

The unit includes a separate isolation facility so that patients who test positive for a transmissible infection can be dialysed safely, without disrupting the main unit. In-house pathology supports the regular blood monitoring that dialysis patients need, and the hospital's ICU and HDU are on the same campus.

Who dialysis is for

Chronic kidney disease that has progressed to kidney failure
Acute kidney injury needing temporary dialysis
Fluid overload that does not respond to medicines
Electrolyte disturbances needing correction
Patients on regular maintenance haemodialysis
Admitted patients who need dialysis during their stay

Services offered

  • Maintenance haemodialysis — for patients on a regular dialysis schedule.
  • Dialysis for admitted patients — including those needing it during an acute illness.
  • Separate isolation dialysis facility — so positive patients can be dialysed safely.
  • In-house pathology — for the routine blood monitoring dialysis requires.
  • ICU and HDU on site — for patients who need closer monitoring around a session.

Diagnostics available on site

Pathology and blood investigations
X-ray and CT scan on site

Frequently asked questions

What is haemodialysis?

Haemodialysis is a treatment that filters waste products and excess fluid from the blood using a machine, when the kidneys can no longer do this adequately. Blood is passed through the machine's filter and returned to the body.

Do you have a separate dialysis facility for positive patients?

Yes. The dialysis unit at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital, Barrackpore has a separate isolation facility so that patients who test positive for a transmissible infection can be dialysed safely, apart from the main unit.

How often will I need dialysis?

That depends on your kidney function, your fluid status and your blood results. Your treating doctor will set a schedule for you and review it as your condition changes. In-house pathology means the blood tests that guide this can be done at the hospital.

Can dialysis patients be admitted if they become unwell?

Yes. The hospital has ICU and HDU critical care on the same campus and a 24×7 emergency department, so a dialysis patient who becomes acutely unwell can be assessed and admitted.

Is treatment in this department available cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government health schemes, PSU empanelments and the major TPAs the hospital is empanelled with. Please carry your scheme or insurance details so the desk can verify eligibility before admission.
