Orthopaedics in Barrackpore

Care for bones, joints and injuries — with C-arm imaging support in theatre, X-ray and CT on site, and a 24×7 emergency department for trauma.

C-arm imaging support X-ray & CT on site 24×7 emergency for trauma

Overview

The orthopaedics department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital looks after bones, joints and the injuries that damage them — from a fracture after a fall to the knee pain that has been building for years. Trauma arriving through the 24×7 emergency department is assessed and, where needed, admitted straight away.

Fracture work is supported by C-arm imaging, which gives the surgeon a live X-ray view during the procedure so that bones and implants can be positioned accurately. X-ray and CT scanning are available in the hospital's own radiology department, and the ICU and HDU are on site for patients with serious injuries.

Conditions we treat

Fractures and injuries after a fall or accident
Knee and hip pain, including arthritis
Back and neck pain
Shoulder pain and frozen shoulder
Sports injuries
Fractures related to weak bones (osteoporosis)

Procedures & services offered

  • Fracture management — including image-guided fixation using C-arm support.
  • Trauma care — through the hospital's 24×7 emergency department.
  • Orthopaedic consultation — for joint, bone and spine problems.
  • Plaster and immobilisation — for injuries that do not need an operation.
  • X-ray and CT on site — so imaging is not delayed.
  • Post-operative care in ICU and HDU — for patients with serious injuries.

Diagnostics available on site

X-ray and CT scan on site
C-arm imaging support during procedures
Pathology and blood investigations

Frequently asked questions

What is C-arm imaging?

A C-arm is a mobile X-ray machine used during a procedure. It gives the surgeon a live X-ray view while they work, which helps in setting a fracture and positioning implants accurately. The orthopaedics department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital has C-arm imaging support.

Can a fracture be treated here at night?

Yes. The emergency department runs 24×7 with triage, so an injury can be assessed at any hour. X-ray and CT are available on site, and patients who need admission can be taken in directly.

Do I need a scan before seeing an orthopaedic surgeon?

Not necessarily. Bring any X-rays or reports you already have. If further imaging is needed, X-ray and CT scanning are available in the hospital's own radiology department, so it can usually be arranged around your consultation.

Who is the orthopaedic surgeon at the hospital?

Dr. Gourab Chatterjee (MBBS, DNB Orthopaedics, MNAMS) consults in the orthopaedics department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital, Barrackpore. Book an appointment and our team will call you back to confirm a slot.

Is treatment in this department available cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government health schemes, PSU empanelments and the major TPAs the hospital is empanelled with. Please carry your scheme or insurance details so the desk can verify eligibility before admission.
