Centres of Excellence

Specialist Departments in Barrackpore

From an emergency at 2am to a planned surgery, the specialities most families need are on one campus at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital — supported by in-house diagnostics, critical care and a 24×7 emergency department.

24×7 emergency with triage In-house diagnostics up to CT ICU & HDU critical care
Our specialities

Care across the specialities, under one roof

Each department works alongside the others, so assessment, diagnosis and treatment can happen in the same place.

Emergency Department

24×7 emergency care with triage, so the most urgent cases are seen first.

Critical Care

ICU and HDU on site for closer monitoring of seriously ill patients.

Cardiology

Echo, ECG, Holter and TMT for heart assessment and follow-up.

NICU & PICU

Intensive care units dedicated to newborns and children.

Gastroenterology

Digestive care with diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopy.

Dialysis

Haemodialysis with a separate isolation facility for positive patients.

ENT

Ear, nose and throat care with microscope-assisted examination.

General Medicine

Assessment and treatment of common and complex medical conditions.

General & Laparoscopic Surgery

Open and keyhole surgery across common surgical conditions.

Orthopaedics

Bone, joint and trauma care with C-arm imaging support.

Gynaecology & Obstetrics

Women's health and pregnancy care with an in-house labour room.

Paediatrics & Neonatology

Care for newborns and children, with a dedicated nursery.

Pulmonology

Assessment and treatment of respiratory and lung conditions.

Urology

Care for kidney, bladder and urinary tract conditions.

Endocrinology

Care for diabetes, thyroid and other hormone-related conditions.

Dentistry

Dental care including oral and maxillofacial surgery.
Why it works together

Why our specialities work together

Departments share the same emergency, diagnostics and critical care backbone — so patients move between them without leaving the hospital.

A 24×7 emergency department with triage supports every speciality at any hour.
In-house diagnostics — pathology, endoscopy, cardiac tests and radiology up to CT.
ICU and HDU critical care on site for patients who need closer monitoring.
Specialists and units share one campus, so referrals stay under one roof.

Not sure which department you need?

Request an appointment and our team will call you back to guide you to the right speciality.
