NICU & PICU in Barrackpore

Dedicated intensive care for newborns and children, with round-the-clock nursing observation and the hospital's 24×7 emergency department alongside.

Neonatal intensive care (NICU) Paediatric intensive care (PICU) 24×7 emergency support

Overview

When a newborn or a child needs more than ward care, they need a unit built around them. Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital has a Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) for newborns and a Paediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU) for infants and children, both on the hospital campus in Barrackpore.

Because the units sit alongside the labour room, the dedicated nursery, the paediatrics department and the 24×7 emergency department, a baby or child who deteriorates can be moved into intensive care without being transferred to another hospital. In-house pathology and radiology support decisions at any hour.

When intensive care may be needed

Babies born early or with a low birth weight
Breathing difficulty in a newborn
Newborn jaundice needing close monitoring
Serious infection in a newborn or child
Feeding difficulty in a newborn
Children needing close observation after serious illness or injury

Services offered

  • Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) — intensive care for newborns who need continuous monitoring.
  • Paediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU) — intensive care for infants and children.
  • Dedicated newborn nursery — for babies who need observation but not intensive care.
  • Round-the-clock nursing observation — in both units.
  • Support from the 24×7 emergency department — for children who arrive acutely unwell.
  • In-house pathology and radiology — so investigations are not delayed.

Diagnostics available on site

Pathology and blood investigations
X-ray and CT scan on site

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the NICU and the PICU?

The NICU (Neonatal Intensive Care Unit) looks after newborn babies, including those born early or with a low birth weight. The PICU (Paediatric Intensive Care Unit) looks after infants and older children who need intensive care. Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital has both.

Do you have both a NICU and a PICU on site?

Yes. Both units are on the hospital campus in Barrackpore, alongside the labour room, the newborn nursery and the paediatrics department, so a baby or child does not need to be transferred elsewhere.

Is intensive care for children available at night?

Yes. Both units run round the clock and are supported by the hospital's 24×7 emergency department, so a child who becomes seriously unwell at any hour can be assessed and admitted.

Is there a nursery for babies who do not need intensive care?

Yes. The hospital has a dedicated newborn nursery for babies who need observation and routine newborn care rather than intensive care.

Is treatment in this department available cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government health schemes, PSU empanelments and the major TPAs the hospital is empanelled with. Please carry your scheme or insurance details so the desk can verify eligibility before admission.
