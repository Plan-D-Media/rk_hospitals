Gynaecology & Obstetrics in Barrackpore

Women's health and pregnancy care, with a labour room in the hospital and a neonatal intensive care unit on the same campus.

In-house labour room NICU on the same campus 24×7 emergency support

Overview

The gynaecology and obstetrics department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital cares for women through pregnancy and for the gynaecological problems that affect them at every stage of life. Antenatal visits, delivery and postnatal care all happen at the hospital in Barrackpore.

The hospital has its own labour room, and a Neonatal Intensive Care Unit on the same campus — so if a baby needs specialist care after birth, that care is a corridor away rather than an ambulance ride. The 24×7 emergency department, ICU and HDU support mothers who need urgent attention.

What we care for

Pregnancy care and antenatal check-ups
Irregular, heavy or painful periods
Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)
Menopause and its symptoms
Pelvic pain
Infections and other gynaecological problems

Procedures & services offered

  • Antenatal (pregnancy) care — regular check-ups through pregnancy.
  • Delivery in the hospital's own labour room — with the neonatal team on the same campus.
  • Newborn support from the NICU — if a baby needs intensive care after birth.
  • Gynaecology consultation — and medical management of gynaecological conditions.
  • Postnatal care — for mother and baby.
  • Support from the 24×7 emergency department — ICU and HDU for urgent problems.

Diagnostics available on site

Pathology and blood investigations
X-ray and CT scan on site

Frequently asked questions

Does the hospital have its own labour room?

Yes. Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital, Barrackpore has an in-house labour room, so antenatal care and delivery can take place at the same hospital.

What happens if my baby needs special care after birth?

The hospital has a Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) and a dedicated newborn nursery on the same campus. A baby who needs intensive care after birth can be moved there without being transferred to another hospital.

Can I have my antenatal check-ups here?

Yes. Antenatal care is provided by the gynaecology and obstetrics department, with in-house pathology for the blood tests that pregnancy care requires.

Who is the gynaecologist at the hospital?

Dr. Debosmita Roy (MBBS) consults in the gynaecology and obstetrics department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital, Barrackpore. Book an appointment and our team will call you back to confirm a slot.

Is treatment in this department available cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government health schemes, PSU empanelments and the major TPAs the hospital is empanelled with. Please carry your scheme or insurance details so the desk can verify eligibility before admission.
