Privacy, Disclaimer & Terms

How Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital, Barrackpore collects and uses the information you share with us, an important note on medical advice, and the terms for using this website.

Privacy Policy Medical Disclaimer Terms of Use

Last updated: to be confirmed.

1. Privacy Policy

Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital — a unit of Sarbani Hospitality and HealthCare Services Pvt Ltd — respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you fill in an appointment request, call-back or enquiry form on this website, we collect the details you choose to give us — typically your name, phone number, the department or service you are enquiring about, a preferred date, and any message you add. We do not ask you to enter detailed medical history or test results through the website.

  • Contact details you submit through our forms (name, phone number, and email if provided).
  • The department, service or health check you are enquiring about.
  • Any message or preferred appointment date you include in the form.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends automatically, such as general usage data collected through website analytics tools.

How we use your information

We use the information you share only to respond to you and to arrange the care or service you have asked about. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Call or message you back to confirm an appointment or answer your enquiry.
  • Route your request to the right department or reception team.
  • Keep a record of your enquiry so we can follow up if needed.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not use the details you submit for unrelated marketing without your consent. Your information may be shared internally with the relevant hospital staff who need it to respond to you, and with service providers who help us operate this website, only to the extent required.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to respond to you and for our internal records.

Your choices

If you would like to know what enquiry information we hold about you, or would like it corrected or removed, please contact us on +91 82408 42519 or visit the hospital reception.

Note: This website privacy policy covers information submitted online. Clinical records created during hospital care are handled separately under the hospital's medical records and confidentiality practices.

2. Medical Disclaimer

The information on this website is provided for general awareness only. It describes the departments, services and facilities available at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital and is intended to help you decide whether to contact us or book an appointment.

  • Content on this website is general information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
  • Always seek the advice of a qualified doctor or other health professional with any questions you have about a medical condition.
  • Never disregard professional medical advice, or delay seeking it, because of something you have read on this website.
  • Reading this website, or sending an enquiry through it, does not create a doctor–patient relationship. That relationship begins only when you are seen and assessed by our clinical team.

In a medical emergency, do not rely on this website. Our emergency department is open 24×7. Call +91 82408 42519 or come directly to the hospital at 203/1, Ghoshpara Road, Barrackpore, Kolkata 700120.

Service availability, timings and facilities may change. We try to keep this website accurate but do not guarantee that every detail is current at all times.
