About Dr. Debosmita Roy

Dr. Debosmita Roy cares for women at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital, Barrackpore, across gynaecology and obstetrics — from routine check-ups and menstrual health to antenatal care through pregnancy. Consultations are supported by the hospital's in-house ultrasound and laboratory, and backed by 24×7 emergency and critical care for reassurance during pregnancy and delivery.

Her qualification is MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery).

Areas of care

Routine gynaecology check-ups
Menstrual & hormonal concerns
Antenatal (pregnancy) care
Family planning & contraception advice
Menopause & women's wellness
Postnatal follow-up
