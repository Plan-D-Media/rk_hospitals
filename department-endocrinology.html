Endocrinology in Barrackpore

Assessment and long-term care for diabetes, thyroid disorders and other hormone-related conditions, with the blood tests that guide them done in-house.

Diabetes, thyroid & hormone care In-house pathology Cashless & TPA

Overview

Hormone problems rarely announce themselves. Tiredness, weight change, thirst or a racing heart can all trace back to the endocrine system. The endocrinology department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital assesses and manages diabetes, thyroid disorders and other hormone-related conditions for patients in and around Barrackpore.

Endocrine care is built on regular blood testing, and the hospital's in-house pathology means those tests can be done and reviewed at the same place you are seen. Where a hormone problem affects the heart, kidneys or eyes, the hospital's other departments and its diagnostics up to CT are on the same campus.

Conditions we treat

Diabetes, and its long-term complications
Thyroid disorders — both underactive and overactive
Obesity and metabolic problems
Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)
Calcium and vitamin D disorders
Other hormone-related conditions

Services offered

  • Assessment and long-term management of diabetes — including review of complications.
  • Thyroid assessment and management — for underactive and overactive thyroid.
  • Management of other hormone-related conditions
  • In-house pathology — for the blood tests that guide endocrine care.
  • Coordination with other departments — when a hormone problem affects the heart, kidneys or eyes.
  • Inpatient care — with ICU and HDU backup where needed.

Diagnostics available on site

Pathology and blood investigations
X-ray and CT scan on site
ECG, Echo, Holter and TMT

Frequently asked questions

Are diabetes blood tests done at the hospital?

Yes. Pathology is available in-house at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital, Barrackpore, so the blood tests used to diagnose and monitor diabetes can be done and reviewed at the hospital.

Do you manage thyroid problems?

Yes. Both an underactive and an overactive thyroid are assessed and managed by the department, with the blood tests that guide treatment available in-house.

How often will I need to be reviewed?

Endocrine conditions such as diabetes and thyroid disorders are long-term, and the review interval depends on how stable your results are. Your doctor will set a schedule and adjust it as your condition changes.

Can diabetes be managed in the General Medicine department instead?

Yes. Diabetes is managed both here and in General Medicine — Dr. Shyan Kumar Biswas holds CCEBDM, a certificate course in evidence-based diabetes management. Book an appointment and our team will guide you to the right clinic.

Is treatment in this department available cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government health schemes, PSU empanelments and the major TPAs the hospital is empanelled with. Please carry your scheme or insurance details so the desk can verify eligibility before admission.
