About Dr. Shyan Kumar Biswas

Dr. Shyan Kumar Biswas is a general medicine specialist at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital, Barrackpore. He looks after adults with a wide range of everyday and long-term health problems — from fever and infections to blood pressure, diabetes and other lifestyle conditions — supported by the hospital's in-house laboratory and imaging, and backed by 24×7 emergency and critical care.

His qualifications include MBBS, MD in General Medicine (Physician) and the CCEBDM certificate course in evidence-based diabetes management.

Areas of care

Fever & common infections
Diabetes assessment & management
High blood pressure (hypertension)
Respiratory & seasonal illness
Health check-up & preventive advice
Follow-up for chronic conditions

Qualifications

  • MBBS — Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery
  • MD (General Medicine) — Doctor of Medicine, Physician
  • CCEBDM — Certificate Course in Evidence-Based Diabetes Management
