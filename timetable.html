Patient guide

Department Timetable & OPD Schedule

Outpatient consultations at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital are by appointment. Please call ahead or request a call back to confirm the current OPD timings for the department and doctor you need.

Consultations by appointment Emergency open 24×7 Call to confirm timings
The emergency department is open 24×7

No appointment is needed for a medical emergency. Call +91 82408 42519 or come straight to the hospital, any hour of the day.

How OPD appointments work

Rather than fixed walk-in hours, outpatient consultations here are arranged by appointment so you are seen at a confirmed time with the right specialist. To book, request a call back through the website or call the hospital directly, and the reception team will confirm the next available slot for your department.
