Paediatrics & Neonatology in Barrackpore

Care for newborns and children, with a dedicated nursery, neonatal and paediatric intensive care, and a 24×7 emergency department.

Dedicated newborn nursery NICU & PICU on site 24×7 emergency support

Overview

The paediatrics and neonatology department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital looks after children from the first minutes of life through to adolescence. Newborns are cared for in a dedicated nursery, and children who fall ill are seen in the paediatric OPD or, when they are more seriously unwell, admitted.

The department has a Neonatal Intensive Care Unit and a Paediatric Intensive Care Unit on the same campus, so a baby or child whose condition worsens can be moved into intensive care immediately. The 24×7 emergency department means a sick child can be seen at any hour, and in-house pathology and radiology support the diagnosis.

Conditions we treat

Fever and childhood infections
Cough, cold and breathing difficulty
Diarrhoea and dehydration
Growth and feeding concerns
Newborn jaundice
Childhood asthma and wheezing

Services offered

  • Paediatric OPD consultation — for children of all ages.
  • Newborn (neonatal) care — in a dedicated nursery.
  • Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) — for newborns who need intensive care.
  • Paediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU) — for infants and children who need intensive care.
  • Inpatient paediatric care — on the wards.
  • 24×7 emergency care — for children who become acutely unwell.

Diagnostics available on site

Pathology and blood investigations
X-ray and CT scan on site

Frequently asked questions

Do you care for newborn babies?

Yes. The hospital has a dedicated newborn nursery, and a Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) for babies who need intensive care. Both are on the same campus as the labour room.

Is there an intensive care unit for children?

Yes. Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital, Barrackpore has a Paediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU) for infants and children, in addition to the NICU for newborns.

Can a sick child be seen at night?

Yes. The emergency department is open 24×7 with triage. A child who becomes seriously unwell out of OPD hours can be assessed and, if needed, admitted to the ward or the PICU.

Are tests for children done at the hospital?

Yes. Pathology and radiology, including X-ray and CT scanning, are available in-house, so investigations for a child can usually be completed at the hospital.

Is treatment in this department available cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government health schemes, PSU empanelments and the major TPAs the hospital is empanelled with. Please carry your scheme or insurance details so the desk can verify eligibility before admission.
