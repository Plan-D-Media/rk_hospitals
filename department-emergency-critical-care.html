Emergency & Critical Care in Barrackpore

Round-the-clock emergency care with triage on arrival, supported by intensive care units and in-house diagnostics — so urgent problems are assessed and treated without delay.

24×7 emergency with triage ICU · HDU · NICU · PICU X-ray & CT on site

Overview

The emergency department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital is open 24 hours a day, every day. Patients are triaged on arrival, which means the most urgent cases are assessed first rather than in the order people walk in. For families in and around Barrackpore, it means serious problems can be looked at close to home, at any hour.

Emergency care works alongside the hospital's critical care units. Patients who need continuous monitoring or organ support can be moved straight into the ICU or HDU, and newborns and children into the NICU and PICU, without being transferred elsewhere. In-house pathology, X-ray and CT mean the first decisions are not held up waiting on an outside lab.

When to come to Emergency

Chest pain or sudden breathlessness
Accidents, injuries and suspected fractures
Sudden weakness, slurred speech or other stroke signs
Severe or sudden abdominal pain
High fever with a serious infection
Sudden collapse or loss of consciousness

Services offered

  • Triage on arrival — every patient is assessed on arrival so the sickest are seen first.
  • Resuscitation and stabilisation — for critically ill and injured patients.
  • Intensive Care Unit (ICU) — for patients who need continuous monitoring and organ support.
  • High Dependency Unit (HDU) — for patients needing closer observation than a general ward provides.
  • NICU and PICU — dedicated intensive care for newborns and children, on the same campus.
  • Direct admission to critical care — from the emergency department, with no transfer to another hospital.

Diagnostics available on site

X-ray and CT scan on site
Pathology and blood investigations
ECG and Echo for cardiac emergencies

Frequently asked questions

Is the emergency department open 24 hours?

Yes. The emergency department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital, Barrackpore runs 24×7, every day of the year, with triage on arrival.

What does triage mean?

Triage is a quick assessment carried out as soon as you arrive. It decides who needs to be seen first based on how serious the problem is, rather than the order in which people arrived. A patient with chest pain will be seen ahead of a patient with a minor injury.

Do you have an ICU?

Yes. The hospital has an Intensive Care Unit (ICU) and a High Dependency Unit (HDU) on site, plus a NICU for newborns and a PICU for children. Patients can be admitted to critical care directly from the emergency department.

What should I bring in an emergency?

If there is time, bring a list of current medicines and known allergies, any previous reports or discharge papers, photo identity, and any insurance, TPA or health-scheme details. Do not delay coming in to collect paperwork.

Is treatment in this department available cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government health schemes, PSU empanelments and the major TPAs the hospital is empanelled with. Please carry your scheme or insurance details so the desk can verify eligibility before admission.
