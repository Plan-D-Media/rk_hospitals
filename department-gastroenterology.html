Gastroenterology & Endoscopy in Barrackpore

Assessment and treatment of digestive and liver problems, with diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopy done on site.

Diagnostic & therapeutic endoscopy In-house pathology & CT 24×7 emergency support

Overview

The gastroenterology department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital looks after problems of the digestive tract and the liver — from long-standing acidity to abdominal pain that needs a closer look. Because endoscopy is available on site, examining the digestive tract does not mean a separate appointment at another centre.

The department is supported by in-house pathology and CT imaging, and by the hospital's 24×7 emergency department and critical care units, so patients who present with bleeding or severe pain can be assessed and admitted quickly.

Conditions we assess

Acidity, reflux and heartburn
Abdominal pain and bloating
Persistent nausea or vomiting
Jaundice and liver-related conditions
A change in bowel habit
Bleeding from the digestive tract

Procedures & services offered

  • Upper GI endoscopy — a camera examination of the food pipe, stomach and the first part of the small intestine.
  • Therapeutic endoscopy — endoscopic procedures that treat, not just examine.
  • Assessment and medical management — of digestive and liver conditions.
  • In-house pathology — for blood and liver investigations.
  • CT imaging on site — when cross-sectional imaging of the abdomen is needed.
  • Inpatient care — with ICU and HDU backup for seriously ill patients.

Frequently asked questions

What is an upper GI endoscopy?

An upper GI endoscopy is an examination in which a thin, flexible tube with a camera is passed through the mouth to look at the food pipe, the stomach and the first part of the small intestine. It helps find the cause of symptoms such as reflux, pain, difficulty swallowing or bleeding.

Is endoscopy done at the hospital itself?

Yes. Diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopy is available in-house at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital, Barrackpore, so you do not need to travel to a separate centre for the procedure.

Do I need to fast before an endoscopy?

You will usually be asked not to eat or drink for some hours before the procedure. The department will give you exact instructions when your endoscopy is scheduled, as the timing depends on the procedure and the time of your appointment.

Can digestive emergencies be handled here?

Yes. The hospital's emergency department runs 24×7 and is backed by ICU and HDU critical care, so problems such as severe abdominal pain or gastrointestinal bleeding can be assessed at any hour and admitted if needed.

Is treatment in this department available cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government health schemes, PSU empanelments and the major TPAs the hospital is empanelled with. Please carry your scheme or insurance details so the desk can verify eligibility before admission.
