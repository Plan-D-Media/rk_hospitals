Who we are

Who We Are — Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital in Barrackpore

The people, the values and the parent company behind a multispeciality hospital built to keep quality care close to Barrackpore.

Our story

A hospital built for Barrackpore

Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital is a unit of Sarbani Hospitality and HealthCare Services Pvt Ltd, based on Ghoshpara Road in Barrackpore, Kolkata.

The hospital was founded by Sarbani Das, who brings 18 years of experience from the hospitality industry. It was started with one aim: to make quality healthcare accessible to families in Barrackpore — the kind of specialist and emergency care that often meant a trip into central Kolkata.

Today the hospital brings 24×7 emergency, complete critical care, 16+ specialities and in-house diagnostics together on one campus, so more of that care can happen close to home.

Read our full About page

Leadership

Sarbani Das, Founder

Sarbani Das founded Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital after 18 years in the hospitality industry. That experience runs through the hospital: alongside clinical care, real attention is paid to how patients and families are treated during a stressful time.

Her aim in setting up the hospital was to make quality, affordable healthcare available to the Barrackpore community — and to run it with the same care for people that hospitality is built on.

What we stand for

Our values

The principles that guide how we look after patients and families in Barrackpore.

Care that puts people first

A hospitality-led approach — patients and families are looked after with attention and dignity, not just treated.

Accessible & local

Quality specialist care kept close to home in Barrackpore, so families don't have to travel into the city.

Affordable by design

Care priced for the community, with cashless treatment for government schemes, PSUs and major TPAs.

Ready around the clock

A 24×7 emergency department with triage, backed by ICU, HDU, NICU and PICU critical care on site.

Specialist-led

Care across 16+ specialities, with investigations up to CT done in-house for faster decisions.

Trusted & transparent

Clear communication with patients and families, and empanelment with schemes and PSUs across the region.

Parent company

Sarbani Hospitality and HealthCare Services Pvt Ltd

Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital operates as a unit of Sarbani Hospitality and HealthCare Services Pvt Ltd. The name reflects the founder's roots in hospitality and the way that thinking is brought into how the hospital is run day to day.

The result is a hospital that treats clinical quality and patient experience as part of the same job — serious care, delivered with genuine attention to the people receiving it.
