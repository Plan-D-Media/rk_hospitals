Patient Guide

Patient Guide & FAQs

Straight answers to the questions families ask most — where we are, how emergency and cashless care work, what we can test and treat on site, and how to book. For anything not covered here, our team is happy to help.

24×7 emergency with triage Cashless & TPA In-house diagnostics up to CT

Frequently asked questions

These answers are for general guidance. Clinical decisions, eligibility and timings are confirmed by the hospital team at the time of your visit.

Where is Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital located?

The hospital is at 203/1, Ghoshpara Road, Airport Area, Barrackpore, Barrackpur Cantonment, Kolkata, West Bengal 700120. You can reach us on +91 82408 42519 or +91 87774 24002.

Do you have a 24×7 emergency department?

Yes. Our emergency department runs 24×7 with triage, so patients are assessed on arrival and the most urgent cases are prioritised at any hour. It is supported by critical care units on site for patients who need closer monitoring.

Is cashless treatment available, and which schemes and TPAs do you accept?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless. We are empanelled with government health schemes such as the West Bengal Health Scheme and Ayushman Bharat, with many public-sector undertakings (PSUs), and with major third-party administrators (TPAs). Please carry your scheme card or insurance details so our desk can verify eligibility before admission.

What diagnostic tests can be done in-house?

Most of the tests your doctor needs are available under one roof — pathology (blood and lab investigations), diagnostic endoscopy, pulmonary function testing, cardiac tests such as ECG, Echo, Holter and TMT, and radiology up to CT scan. Doing tests on site means faster reporting and quicker decisions on your care.

What critical care units does the hospital have?

We have complete critical care on site — an Intensive Care Unit (ICU) and High Dependency Unit (HDU) for adults, plus a Neonatal ICU (NICU) and Paediatric ICU (PICU) for newborns and children.

How do I book an appointment?

You can request an appointment using the form on our appointment page, or call +91 82408 42519 or +91 87774 24002. You can also message us on WhatsApp. Our team will call back to confirm a suitable slot.

Which specialities and departments are available?

The hospital offers 16+ specialities, including Emergency & Trauma, Cardiology, Critical Care, Gastroenterology, ENT, General Medicine, General & Laparoscopic Surgery, Orthopaedics, Gynaecology & Obstetrics, Paediatrics & Neonatology, Pulmonology, Urology, Endocrinology, Nephrology / Dialysis and Dentistry. You can browse them on the specialities page.

Do you have an ambulance service?

For urgent help, call our emergency line on +91 82408 42519 and the team will guide you on the fastest way to reach the hospital.

What are the visiting hours and attendant policy?

Visiting arrangements differ between general wards and critical care units, where access is more restricted for patient safety. Please check the current visiting times and attendant policy with the front desk during your visit.

Are preventive health check-up packages available?

Yes. Preventive health check-up packages can be built around your age and needs, using our in-house diagnostics. Inclusions and prices are being finalised — see the health check-up page or contact us for the latest details.
