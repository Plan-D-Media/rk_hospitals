General Medicine in Barrackpore

Physician-led assessment and treatment of common and complex medical conditions, with pathology, ECG, X-ray and CT available in-house.

Physician OPD & inpatient care In-house pathology, ECG, X-ray & CT 24×7 emergency support

Overview

General Medicine is where most medical journeys begin. The department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital assesses and treats the illnesses that bring people to hospital most often — fevers and infections, diabetes, high blood pressure, thyroid problems, chest infections — and decides when a specialist opinion or admission is needed.

Because pathology, ECG, X-ray and CT are all available in-house, a physician can investigate and start treatment during the same visit rather than sending you away for tests. Patients who need admission are looked after on the wards, with ICU and HDU on site if their condition needs closer monitoring.

Conditions we treat

Fever and infections
Diabetes and its complications
High blood pressure (hypertension)
Thyroid and other hormone-related problems
Chest infections and respiratory illness
Anaemia, weakness and unexplained weight loss

Services offered

  • Physician OPD consultation — for new problems and for long-term conditions.
  • Management of chronic conditions — such as diabetes, hypertension and thyroid disease.
  • Pre-operative medical fitness assessment — before planned surgery.
  • Inpatient care — on the wards, with ICU and HDU backup.
  • Health check-ups — with the hospital's in-house pathology.
  • Referral to the right specialist — when a problem needs one.

Diagnostics available on site

Pathology and blood investigations
ECG, Echo, Holter and TMT
X-ray and CT scan on site

Frequently asked questions

What kind of conditions does General Medicine treat?

General Medicine covers the broad range of adult medical illness — fevers and infections, diabetes, high blood pressure, thyroid disorders, chest infections, anaemia and unexplained symptoms. The physician assesses the problem, arranges investigations and either treats it or refers you to the right specialist.

Is diabetes care available in this department?

Yes. Diabetes is one of the conditions managed by the General Medicine department. Dr. Shyan Kumar Biswas holds MBBS, MD (Physician) and CCEBDM, a certificate course in evidence-based diabetes management. In-house pathology means the blood tests that guide diabetes care can be done at the hospital.

Can I get my tests done on the same visit?

In most cases, yes. Pathology, ECG, X-ray and CT scanning are all available in-house at the hospital in Barrackpore, so your physician can order investigations and review the reports without sending you to an outside centre.

Do I need an appointment?

For OPD, we recommend booking an appointment so we can confirm a slot with the physician and keep your waiting time down. Our team will call you back to confirm. If the problem is urgent, the emergency department is open 24×7.

Is treatment in this department available cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government health schemes, PSU empanelments and the major TPAs the hospital is empanelled with. Please carry your scheme or insurance details so the desk can verify eligibility before admission.
