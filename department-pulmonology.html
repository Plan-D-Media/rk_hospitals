Pulmonology in Barrackpore

Assessment and treatment of lung and breathing problems, with pulmonary function testing and bronchoscopy available in-house.

PFT & bronchoscopy in-house Chest X-ray & CT on site ICU & HDU support

Overview

The pulmonology department at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital assesses and treats problems of the lungs and airways — the long-standing cough, the breathlessness that has crept up over months, the chest infection that will not clear. Pulmonary function testing measures how well the lungs are working, and bronchoscopy allows the airways to be examined directly.

Both are available at the hospital, along with chest X-ray and CT imaging, so a breathing problem can be investigated without being sent from one centre to another. Patients who need closer monitoring have the hospital's ICU and HDU on the same campus, and the 24×7 emergency department handles acute breathlessness at any hour.

Asthma

Procedures & services offered

  • Pulmonary function testing (PFT) — to measure how well the lungs are working.
  • Bronchoscopy — a camera examination of the airways.
  • Assessment and medical management — of asthma, COPD and other respiratory conditions.
  • Chest X-ray and CT imaging — in the hospital's own radiology department.
  • Inpatient care — with ICU and HDU for patients who need closer monitoring.
  • 24×7 emergency care — for acute breathlessness.

Frequently asked questions

What is a pulmonary function test (PFT)?

A PFT is a breathing test. You breathe into a machine that measures how much air your lungs can hold and how quickly you can move it in and out. It helps diagnose and monitor conditions such as asthma and COPD. PFT is available in-house at the hospital.

Is bronchoscopy done at the hospital?

Yes. Bronchoscopy — a camera examination of the airways — is available in-house at Ramakrishna Multispeciality Hospital, Barrackpore, so you do not need to be referred to a separate centre for it.

Can I have a CT scan of my chest here?

Yes. Chest X-ray and CT scanning are available in the hospital's own radiology department, which means imaging and consultation can usually be arranged around the same visit.

What if I become acutely breathless?

Come to the emergency department, which is open 24×7 with triage. Patients who need closer monitoring can be admitted to the hospital's ICU or HDU on the same campus.

Is treatment in this department available cashless?

Eligible patients can be treated cashless under government health schemes, PSU empanelments and the major TPAs the hospital is empanelled with. Please carry your scheme or insurance details so the desk can verify eligibility before admission.
